Scoring Breakdown
Decision model: Weighted scoring — Sea State 30 pts · Wind 22 pts · Regional Threats 18 pts · Precip/Visibility 10 pts · Swell Period 10 pts · Lightning/T-Storm 10 pts · Daylight 6 pts · Tidal State 4 pts = 100 pts. Scored at your planned survey time. GO ≥68 · CAUTION 45–67 · NO-GO <45. Hard fails: wave/wind exceeds vessel limit, confirmed active thunderstorm (T-storm prob ≥70%), or nighttime (if daytime-only enabled). Regional threat score deducts only for genuinely close cyclone/quake events — global intel feeds are informational only. Sources: Open-Meteo Marine (ERA5+GFS-Wave), Open-Meteo GFS (CAPE/LPI), NOAA, GDACS, USGS, ReliefWeb. Not a substitute for official briefings.

Ambient Acoustic Noise Estimate
Sea-surface noise floor for MBES/SSS based on Knudsen curves (Beaufort). Affects shallow-water depth detection & side-scan range.
Ref: Knudsen et al. 1948; Wenz 1962. NL estimated at 1 kHz reference. Higher sea states elevate ambient noise 6 dB/octave below 1 kHz.

Vessel Motion Risk — Data Quality Impact
Estimated heave amplitude and roll angle affecting MBES beam geometry and positioning accuracy.
Ref: NOAA NOS hydrographic survey standing orders require heave ≤0.2m and roll ≤2° for Exclusive/Special Order. Values estimated from Hs, period, and vessel class.
